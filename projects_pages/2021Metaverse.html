Metaverse Map Production Contest

Yonsei University · 2021

Undergrad Competition 🏆 1st place

Each space was designed and divided according to a specific theme and purpose. For example, users who need a small-group meeting can use the Truth Gallery, which contains multiple meeting rooms with limited capacity. By providing spaces tailored to different needs and purposes, the entire Yonsei Literature Museum is expected to function as an active virtual gathering space, particularly for remote interaction. Through this project, I gained experience in spatial planning and designing virtual environments that facilitate effective user communication on the Gather Town platform.
