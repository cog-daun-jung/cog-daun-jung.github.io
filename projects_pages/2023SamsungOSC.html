Samsung Open Source Companions

Extracurricular activity · 2023

Undergrad Extracurricular Activity

Served as a student Companion promoting Samsung Open Source's tools and use cases — producing social content and presenting as a student representative at SDC Korea 2023.

Instagram Contents

As a member of the Samsung Open Source Companion Instagram Team, I helped communicate technical updates and stories about people involved in Samsung Open Source through engaging visual content, including Korean initial-consonant quizzes and silhouette quizzes designed to capture public interest. I participated throughout the content planning and design process.

Youtube Contents

As a member of the Samsung Open Source Companion YouTube Team, I worked on creating videos that made technical concepts accessible and easy to understand for a general audience. I contributed to various aspects of the project, including thumbnail design, video planning, filming, and on-camera presentation.
